# LAPaL — patent landscape of long-acting therapeutics

> Patent status of 124 long-acting medicines, formulations and technologies in low- and middle-income countries: granted, filed (pending) and no-longer-in-force patents, expected expiry dates and voluntary licences, by product and by country. Published by the Medicines Patent Pool (MPP). Data snapshot: 2026-10-27.

Patent status comes from MedsPaL (https://www.medspal.org) as linked to each LAPaL entry, or, where marked preliminary, from the patent cards on the LAPaL entry page. Status is "as recorded"; future dates are projections from expected expiry dates. Voluntary licences "may allow" generic manufacturing and/or sale in some territories; the licence terms decide. Patent information is for information only, not legal advice. Please quote the snapshot date with any figure.
