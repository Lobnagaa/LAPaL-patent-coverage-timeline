Questions and answers

In which countries is Semaglutide patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Semaglutide are granted in 19 low- and middle-income countries (Algeria, Brazil, China, Indonesia, Iran, Islamic Rep., Iraq, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, North Macedonia, Pakistan and 7 others) and filed in 6, according to MedsPaL. No patent is recorded in 96 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Semaglutide patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Semaglutide is 13 July 2043. Expected expiry by family: WO2005027978 (compound): 17 September 2024; WO2006097537 (compound): 20 March 2026; WO2014005858 (use): 21 June 2033; WO2019038412 (composition): 24 August 2038; WO2019072941 (dose/regimen, use): 10 October 2038; WO2021144477 (composition): 17 February 2041.
