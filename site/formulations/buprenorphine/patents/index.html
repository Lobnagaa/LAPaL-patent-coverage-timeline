Questions and answers

In which countries is Buprenorphine patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Buprenorphine are granted in 17 low- and middle-income countries (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Brazil, China, Colombia, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Mexico, Peru, Serbia and 5 others) and filed in 2, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 111 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Buprenorphine patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Buprenorphine is 26 July 2033. Expected expiry by family: WO2013083460 (formulation): 28 November 2032; WO2014016428 (formulation): 26 July 2033. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
