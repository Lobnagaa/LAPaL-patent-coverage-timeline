Questions and answers

In which countries is CD388 patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for CD388 are granted in 3 low- and middle-income countries (China, Colombia and South Africa) and filed in 28, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 82 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.

The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for CD388 is 8 September 2040. Expected expiry by family: WO1991016320 (compound): 15 November 2011; WO2021046549 (compound, combination): 8 September 2040. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
