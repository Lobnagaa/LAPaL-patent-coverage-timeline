Questions and answers

In which countries is Gemcitabine Intravesical LAI patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Gemcitabine Intravesical LAI are granted in 11 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Brazil, China, India, Kazakhstan, Mexico, North Macedonia, Serbia, South Africa, Turkiye and Viet Nam) and filed in 0, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 118 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Gemcitabine Intravesical LAI patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Gemcitabine Intravesical LAI is 19 August 2034. Expected expiry by family: US4808614 (compound): 5 May 2010; WO2010151893, WO2010151896 (device): 28 June 2030; WO2011084712 (device): 17 December 2030; WO2015026813 (device): 19 August 2034.
