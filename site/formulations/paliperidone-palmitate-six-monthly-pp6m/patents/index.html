Questions and answers

In which countries is Paliperidone Palmitate Six-Monthly (PP6M) patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Paliperidone Palmitate Six-Monthly (PP6M) are granted in 12 low- and middle-income countries (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, China, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Mexico, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tajikistan, Turkiye and Turkmenistan) and filed in 0, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 117 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Paliperidone Palmitate Six-Monthly (PP6M) patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Paliperidone Palmitate Six-Monthly (PP6M) is 24 November 2041. Expected expiry by family: WO2022111858 (dosing regimen): 7 May 2041; WO2022111859 (dosing regimen): 7 May 2041; WO2022112367 (process): 24 November 2041. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
