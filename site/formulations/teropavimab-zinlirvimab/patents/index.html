Questions and answers

In which countries is Teropavimab + Zinlirvimab patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Teropavimab + Zinlirvimab are granted in 13 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, China, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Tajikistan, Turkiye and 1 others) and filed in 1, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 117 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Teropavimab + Zinlirvimab patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Teropavimab + Zinlirvimab is 12 September 2039. Expected expiry by family: WO2012158948 (active substance): 17 May 2032; WO2014063059 (active substance): 18 October 2033; WO2020056145 (combination of active substances): 12 September 2039. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
