Questions and answers

In which countries is Insulin Efsitora patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Insulin Efsitora are granted in 34 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Algeria, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Botswana, Brazil, China, Colombia, Ghana, India and 22 others) and filed in 27, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 70 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Insulin Efsitora patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Insulin Efsitora is 14 November 2042. Expected expiry by family: WO2016178905 (compound): 28 April 2025; WO2022132709 (method of treatment): 14 December 2041; WO2022132712 (method of treatment): 14 December 2041; WO2023086980 (formulation): 14 November 2042.
