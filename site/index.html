LAPaL patent coverage

Data snapshot 27 October 2026 · Prototype by the Medicines Patent Pool for LAPaL

Where long-acting medicines, formulations and technologies are patented in low- and middle-income countries, when those patents are expected to expire, and where voluntary licences may allow generic manufacturing and/or sale. Patent data come from MedsPaL as linked to each LAPaL entry, or, where marked preliminary, from the patent cards on lapal.ch.

This is a working prototype for the LAPaL developers. The official feature will be built on lapal.ch.
