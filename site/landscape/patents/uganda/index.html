Patents in Uganda for HIV long-acting therapeutics

Low income · Data snapshot 27 October 2026 · Download CSV

As of 27 October 2026, of the 19 HIV long-acting products on LAPaL with MedsPaL patent data, 0 have at least one patent granted in Uganda, 5 have patents filed (pending) only, 9 have patents that are no longer in force, and 5 have no patent recorded there.

The latest expected expiry among granted or filed patents is 18 January 2042 (lepetegravir (GS-1720)).

Voluntary licences. Uganda is in the territory of the cabotegravir MPP licence (ViiV Healthcare), lenacapavir bilateral licence (Gilead Sciences) and alimatravir bilateral licence (MSD). These licences may allow generic manufacturing and/or sale in some territories, whatever the patent status shown; check the licence terms.
