Patents in Kyrgyz Republic for HIV long-acting therapeutics

Lower-middle income · Data snapshot 27 October 2026 · Download CSV

As of 27 October 2026, of the 19 HIV long-acting products on LAPaL with MedsPaL patent data, 12 have at least one patent granted in Kyrgyz Republic, 0 have patents filed (pending) only, 6 have patents that are no longer in force, and 1 has no patent recorded there.

The latest expected expiry among granted or filed patents is 18 January 2042 (lepetegravir (GS-1720)).

Voluntary licences. Kyrgyz Republic is in the territory of the cabotegravir MPP licence (ViiV Healthcare), lenacapavir bilateral licence (Gilead Sciences) and alimatravir bilateral licence (MSD). These licences may allow generic manufacturing and/or sale in some territories, whatever the patent status shown; check the licence terms.

Questions and answers

Which HIV long-acting medicines are patented in Kyrgyz Republic?
As of 27 October 2026, MedsPaL records at least one granted patent in Kyrgyz Republic for 12 of the 19 HIV long-acting products on LAPaL with patent data: Bictegravir (BIC), Cabotegravir, Cabotegravir & Lenacapavir, Cabotegravir + Rilpivirine, Drug Combination Nanoparticles (DcNP), fremocapavir (VH-499), Lenacapavir (LEN), Lenacapavir 6-Monthly, lepetegravir (GS-1720), MK-8591D Islatravir + Lenacapavir, Rilpivirine 2-monthly and Tenofovir-Lamivudine-Dolutegravir (TLD) - long-acting injectable (LAI) (TLD LAI).
Do voluntary licences cover Kyrgyz Republic?
Yes. Kyrgyz Republic is in the territory of the cabotegravir MPP licence from ViiV Healthcare, lenacapavir bilateral licence from Gilead Sciences and alimatravir bilateral licence from MSD, which may allow generic manufacturing and/or sale. Check the licence terms.
