Questions and answers

In which countries is Nanocrystalline Prodrug Technology patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Nanocrystalline Prodrug Technology are granted in 9 low- and middle-income countries (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, China, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Mexico, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan) and filed in 5, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 108 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Nanocrystalline Prodrug Technology patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Nanocrystalline Prodrug Technology is 22 October 2039. Expected expiry by family: WO2020086555 (compound/formulation/method of treatment): 22 October 2039. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
