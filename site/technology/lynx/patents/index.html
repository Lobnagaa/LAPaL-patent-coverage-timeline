Questions and answers

In which countries is LYNX patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for LYNX are granted in 4 low- and middle-income countries (Brazil, China, Mexico and South Africa) and filed in 5, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 122 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do LYNX patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for LYNX is 8 June 2038. Expected expiry by family: WO2015191920 (device): 11 June 2035; WO2017070612 (device): 21 October 2036; WO2018227147 (device): 8 June 2038. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
