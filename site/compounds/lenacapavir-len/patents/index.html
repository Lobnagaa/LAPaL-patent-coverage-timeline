Questions and answers

In which countries is Lenacapavir (LEN) patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Lenacapavir (LEN) are granted in 63 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Benin, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Botswana, Brazil, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 51 others) and filed in 7, according to MedsPaL. Expected expiry by family: WO2014134566 (compound): 28 February 2034; WO2018035359 (compound): 17 August 2037; WO2019035904 (polymorphs): 16 August 2038; WO2019161280 (intermediate(s), process): 15 February 2039; WO2020018459 (use): 15 July 2039.
