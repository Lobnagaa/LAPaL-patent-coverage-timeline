Questions and answers

In which countries is Efruxifermin (mAb) patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Efruxifermin (mAb) are granted in 19 low- and middle-income countries (Algeria, Argentina, Brazil, China, Colombia, India, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Lebanon, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco and 7 others) and filed in 32, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 78 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Efruxifermin (mAb) patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Efruxifermin (mAb) is 12 October 2042. Expected expiry by family: WO2009149171 (compound): 3 June 2029; WO2010129503 (not provided): 4 May 2030; WO2013033452 (method of treatment): 30 August 2032; WO2023064808 (formulation): 12 October 2042. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
