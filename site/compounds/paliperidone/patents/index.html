Questions and answers

In which countries is Paliperidone patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Paliperidone are granted in 16 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Mexico, Moldova, North Macedonia, Philippines, Serbia and 4 others) and filed in 4, according to MedsPaL. No patent is recorded in 76 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Paliperidone patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Paliperidone is 17 December 2028. Expected expiry by family: CA2000786 (compound): 16 October 2009; WO9744039 (composition): 12 May 2017; WO9925354 (composition): 10 November 2018; WO2009080651 (dose/regimen): 17 December 2028. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
