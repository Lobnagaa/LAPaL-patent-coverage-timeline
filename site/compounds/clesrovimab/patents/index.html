Questions and answers

In which countries is Clesrovimab patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Clesrovimab are granted in 30 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Botswana, Brazil, China, Colombia, Dominican Republic, Georgia, Ghana and 18 others) and filed in 17, according to MedsPaL.
