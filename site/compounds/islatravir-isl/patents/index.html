Questions and answers

In which countries is Islatravir (ISL) patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Islatravir (ISL) are granted in 25 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Botswana, Dominican Republic, Georgia, Ghana, Iran, Islamic Rep., Jordan, Kazakhstan and 13 others) and filed in 8, according to MedsPaL.
