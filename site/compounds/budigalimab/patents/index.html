Questions and answers

In which countries is Budigalimab patented?
As of 27 October 2026, patents for Budigalimab are granted in 9 low- and middle-income countries (Albania, China, Colombia, Mexico, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, South Africa and Turkiye) and filed in 2, according to LAPaL patent cards (preliminary). No patent is recorded in 111 of the 131 low- and middle-income countries.
When do Budigalimab patents expire?
The latest expected expiry of a granted or filed patent for Budigalimab is 14 September 2037. Expected expiry by family: WO2018053106 (compound): 14 September 2037; WO2025038861 (method of treatment): 15 August 2044. Expiry dates can change, for example with term extensions or litigation.
